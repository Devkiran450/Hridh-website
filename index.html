Why HRIDH

Originality over repetition

Most fashion today is produced in large quantities. Patterns are repeated. Designs are replicated. HRIDH creates original textile artworks that exist only once. Each piece is individually hand-painted and permanently retired after purchase. You are not buying a design. You are acquiring the only version of it that will ever exist.

About HRIDH

HRIDH is a contemporary wearable art label focused on originality. Each artwork begins as a blank fabric and is individually painted by hand. No templates are used. No designs are repeated. The intention is simple: Create textile artworks that cannot be replicated. HRIDH exists for individuals who value expression, originality, and ownership of rare objects. Not mass produced. Not restocked. Not repeated.
